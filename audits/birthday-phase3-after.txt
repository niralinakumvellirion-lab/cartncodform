═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW "FIRST ORDER" IS DECIDED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In POST /cod-order, after the order document is saved:
  const count = await CodOrder.countDocuments({ shopDomain: shop, phone });
  isFirstOrder = (count === 1);

count===1 means only the just-created order exists for that phone in that shop.
Returned to the COD form client in the response body. The form JS shows the
birthday capture UI when postOrderEnabled && isFirstOrder && !localStorage saved.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4b FEASIBILITY (thank-you page checkout UI extension)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NOT BUILT. Feasibility finding:

Shopify checkout UI extensions (purchase.thank-you.block.render) can make
network requests and receive a session token. However, to match the customer
in our Profile DB we need their phone or email. Checkout UI extensions do not
expose customer contact fields without the protected data scopes
  • customer_privacy
  • personal_customer_data_opt_in
These require a Partner dashboard submission and review process — they cannot
be self-approved. Without a Profile match we cannot:
  - Suppress the form for customers who already saved a birthday
  - Identify whose birthday to store

Decision: build nothing. The post-order method (Method 3) via the COD form
covers the same intent for COD customers who are the primary audience.
A thank-you extension can be added in a future phase once protected scopes
are approved.
