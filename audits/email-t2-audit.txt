─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IDOR / SECURITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All /:id routes: findById result is rejected (404) when
  template.shopDomain !== req.shopDomain. A shop can never read, preview,
  patch, delete, or send via another shop's template.

Send: Profile.findOne({ _id, shopDomain: req.shopDomain }) — a shop cannot
  send to another shop's customer profile.

shopDomain never written from request body — set once at create from
  req.shopDomain, excluded from EDITABLE_FIELDS in PATCH.
