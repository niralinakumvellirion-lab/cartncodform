DEVIATION - interpretation of Change 4's "product-specific" condition
=========================================================================
The task's exact wording: "Show the thumbnail only on suggestions that
are product-specific (Cart & Purchase category, or any suggestion
where the customer has a top product)." Two readings are possible:
  (a) thumbnail shows when EITHER the card's category is Cart &
      Purchase, OR (independently, any category) the customer has a
      top product — i.e. an OR of two independent triggers, which in
      practice collapses to "show whenever hasTopProduct is true"
      (since that alone covers every category once true), with Cart &
      Purchase as a redundant special case that would ALSO show a
      (placeholder) thumbnail even for a customer with NO top product
      at all, purely because of category.
  (b) thumbnail shows only on the Cart & Purchase category, further
      narrowed to only when the customer additionally has a top
      product.
Implemented reading (a) — the literal OR — since the sentence's
grammar reads as two alternatives ("X, or Y"), not a further
restriction of X by Y. Net effect: every suggestion card in the Cart &
Purchase category always shows a thumbnail (image today, always the
grey placeholder since imageUrl is never populated); every suggestion
card in any OTHER category also shows one whenever the selected
customer has a real (non-numeric-ID) top product title. Flagging this
explicitly since the alternate reading (b) would have produced
visibly different behavior (no thumbnail at all on Offers/Re-
engagement/Post Purchase suggestions, regardless of the customer's
product history) and the task's phrasing does not unambiguously rule
it out.
