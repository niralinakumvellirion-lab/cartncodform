events of ANY of the 4 listed types combined), a very
activity-heavy customer could have their product_view events pushed
out of that 20-event window by other event types, meaning
topProducts reflects "products viewed within the last ~20 tracked
timeline events" rather than "top 3 products this customer has ever
viewed."
