(a) Body-size limit — can two base64 images (~1.5 MB each) pass?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

backend/server.js line 71: express.json({ limit: '10mb', … })

App-wide global limit is 10 MB. Two 1.5 MB images → ~3 MB of base64
(base64 inflates ×4/3 → ~2 MB raw → fine within 10 MB even before any
queue-specific override). No per-route body limit exists on the queue
router. Conclusion: 10 MB global limit is sufficient; no per-route
increase is required.

mobileImageUrl or desktopImageUrl in state.
