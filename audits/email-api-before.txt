=== express.json BODY-SIZE LIMIT (backend/server.js:71) ===

  limit: '10mb'

  An 8 MB binary image encoded as base64 → ~10.67 MB of JSON.
  10 MB global limit is BELOW what the /photo route needs.
  The global limit must be respected (spec says don't raise it).
  A route-scoped express.json({ limit: '11mb' }) is required — but note:
  with Express 4, the global app.use() body parser runs before route
  middleware, so it will reject >10 MB requests before the route handler
  runs. This gap is documented and must be noted in the self-audit.
