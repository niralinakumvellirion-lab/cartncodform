=== KNOWN LIMITATION ===

express.json({limit:'11mb'}) on POST /photo is route-scoped, but Express 4 runs the global
app.use(express.json({limit:'10mb'})) in server.js first. Requests larger than ~10 MB
are therefore rejected at the global layer before the route handler runs (HTTP 413 from
Express, not our handler). The 8 MB image-content cap (encoded ~10.67 MB) can't be fully
enforced from inside the handler; in production the effective cap is ~7.5 MB binary.
This gap is documented in the pre-audit and was acknowledged as out-of-scope for Phase E2.
A future fix is to raise or remove the global limit, or decode the dataUrl in a streaming
pre-filter.
