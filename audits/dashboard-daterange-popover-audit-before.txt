PRE-AUDIT: three fixes on DashboardScreen.jsx
2026-10-02

=== 1. What drives the default date range ===
Line 702: const [dateFilter, setDateFilter] = useState('7d');
Plain useState with hardcoded '7d'. No URL read, no sessionStorage.
When the user navigates away and comes back, React unmounts/remounts and
the component re-initialises to '7d' regardless of what was selected.
customRange at line 703 is also plain useState({ from: null, to: null }).

=== 2. What clips the popover ===
The popover is rendered as position:absolute inside the funnel step card,
which is inside the left-column scroll container:
  line 1381: <div style={{ flex:1, overflowY:'auto', ... }}>
Any position:absolute descendant of an overflowY:auto container is clipped
at that container's boundary. The popover escapes the step card (no
overflow:hidden on the card itself) but is clipped at the scroll-container
edge, making it invisible or half-visible when it extends downward.
Secondary issue: z-index 50 is inside the step card's stacking context and
may be painted over by later sibling cards in the grid.

=== 3. Current card layout ===
Top div: flex row, label + info span with marginBottom:6
Then number div, then bar div, then rate text div — all in document order,
no alignment to a shared baseline. Cards with longer labels push the
number further down, so numbers don't line up across the five cards.
