FIRSTSEEN FIELD REPORT
- `profile.createdAt` is used as "First Seen" (mapped in formatCDDate).
- The journey endpoint (`/api/events/:shop/journey?profileId=...`) does NOT
  return a `firstSeen` or `firstSeenAt` field on profile documents.
- The Profile model likely populates `createdAt` (Mongoose default) but
  `firstSeenAt` is not set, so the field renders empty in production.
  Using `profile.createdAt` is a best-available proxy; a backend field
  fix is out of scope here.
