5. GUARD AGAINST DOUBLE SUBMIT
============================================================

Today: `allow.disabled = true` at Stage 1 prevents a second click executing the
handler body, because `disabled` prevents native click events on buttons.
However, `allow.disabled` is checked inside the handler itself (not at the top).
The spec requires an explicit early-return guard.
