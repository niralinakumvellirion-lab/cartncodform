    (desktop card: position:fixed,top:50%,left:50%,transform:translate(-50%,-50%))

  ccf-push.js:2817  'background:linear-gradient(145deg,#ffffff,#f8f9ff)'
    (mobile card: position:fixed,bottom:24px — the bottom-anchored variant)

for the SPLIT layout (right panel bg = cfg.bgColor, ccf-push.js:3193).

"classic split: E+U; classic card: E*".

image+content stacked layout but the card layout is what phones get when
layout === 'card'). Phones NEVER see cfg.bgColor applied.

IMPLICATION FOR TASK:
  Task item 3 says: "Classic CARD layout must use cfg.bgColor (when
  bgColor is the default #FFFFFF keep today's gradient)".
  Implementation: replace the two HC gradient strings with:
    bg === '#FFFFFF' || bg === '#ffffff' || !bg
      ? 'linear-gradient(145deg,#ffffff,#f8f9ff)'
      : bg
  And adapt sub-text / branding colours via fgOn(bg) when dark.
